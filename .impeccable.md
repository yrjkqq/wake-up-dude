## Design Context

### Users
The target users are those who have "difficulty getting up" in the morning. Usage scenarios mainly occur in bed, in an extremely sleepy, blurry-eyed, and usually pitch-black environment. The core demand of the user is to be effectively woken up, while getting a hint of interactive experience with emotional value (or quirkiness) during this painful process.

### Brand Personality
Minimal, Apple-like/Premium, Quirky/Playful.
While ensuring top-tier interface texture and smoothness, it inherently exudes an unconventional humor and neurosis.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Theme**: Reference Spotify's design language. Clear at a glance, extremely high-contrast typography, thick and visually impactful sans-serif fonts, removing all unnecessary decorations.
- **Color & Theme**: Forcible Pure Dark Mode. The background is deep, and the text is highlighted and clear but gentle, taking care of the blurry vision of eyes just opened, absolutely avoiding glaring bright colors.
- **Anti-references**: Absolutely avoid using the style of Windows/Microsoft platform software, reject dull enterprise-level UI, rigid square grids, or uniform complex border structures.
- **Animation Design**: Add transition animations full of flow experience, especially on the `<SwipeToStop>` sliding unlock component, it needs to provide a smooth feedback like a physical spring, combined with realistic phone haptics.

### Design Principles
1. **Typography as UI**: Use extra-large typography and breathing white space to build hierarchy, abandoning traditional card or border constraints.
2. **Late Night Gentle High Contrast**: Stick to pure black background without impurities, paired with clear and gentle high-contrast bright text, allowing sleepy users to grasp the key points instantly.
3. **Silky Physical Touch**: Interactions must have physical feedback, giving core functions like sliding unlock top-tier Fluid Animation and dynamic rebound response.
4. **Restrained Humor**: The visual skin maintains an "Apple-like" restraint and premium feel, leaving the "quirkiness" to the highly tensioned copy typography and unpredictable AI voices.
